GEAR & collection

Clearing songs unlocks cosmetic items one by one. The game never tells you where each one comes from, so here is the full list.

  • Difficulty conditions mean that difficulty or higher: a HARD condition also opens with an EXTREME or PERFECT clear.
  • GEAR is worked out from your records each time. New items pop up on the result screen.
  • Plates, pins and title words stay once earned, even if a city gets more songs later.

Items the game keeps secret are blurred. Tap to reveal.
