STEP UP courses

Certification courses of three songs in a row. Fail a song or miss its grade and the course ends there. Passing adds the course bonus to your rating (0.50 at most in total).

  • Every song must be cleared; where a grade is listed, you need that grade (including + or ++) or better.
  • The first five courses (SPARK–RESONANCE) give 0.05–0.10; the three pack courses give 0.03–0.04.
  • VELOCITY, OVERLOAD and ZENITH each include one JAKARTA, SAPPORO or NEO AKIBA song, so they need that pack.
