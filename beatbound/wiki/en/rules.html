Score & Rank

Score & rank rules

You move a cube on an 8×8 pad board and dodge blasts that go off with the music. You score for each blast you dodge, for COOL (dodging right next to a blast) and for JUST (slipping out just before it goes off).

The 1,000,000-point scale

Each chart has its own unit u. 1,000,000 is the best run with no hits, every COOL and no JUST.

Max COOL is the exact optimum found by a bot that knows the whole chart (50 ms grid, at least 150 ms between inputs, one cell per move). Each song page lists its values.

FEVER

Every song has one FEVER stretch, its hottest part. COOL and JUST score double there. Each song page shows where it is.
