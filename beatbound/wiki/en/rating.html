Rating

Rating is a skill number built from your 20 best charts. Clearing harder charts with higher scores raises it. Wins, losses and play count do not count.

Formula

chart value = internal level × min(1, score ÷ 1,000,000) (full clears only; fails and mid-song starts = 0) rating = (sum of your 1st–5th chart values) × 1/10 + (sum of your 6th–20th chart values) × 1/30 + STEP UP bonus (up to 0.50) + ladder season bonus (up to 0.50)
  • A chart slot is song × EASY/NORMAL/HARD/EXTREME. Each slot keeps only its best record.
  • PERFECT is the EXTREME chart where one hit ends the run, so it shares the EXTREME slot; the higher of the two counts.
  • The weights add up to 5×1/10 + 15×1/30 = 1, so 20 slots all at value x give a rating of x.
  • Empty slots count as 0, so fewer than 20 records means a lower rating.
  • Scores above 1,000,000 (from JUST) still cap the factor at 1.
  • The number on screen is cut, not rounded, at two decimals: 9.999 shows as 9.99.
  • Ladder and friend-room runs count like normal runs. Winning or losing a match does not move your rating.

Example

Clear Pixelated EXTREME (internal 14.507) with 950,000 and its chart value is 14.507 × 0.95 = 13.782. As your 1st chart it adds 1.378 to your rating; from 6th down it adds 0.459.

The top 5 slots make up half of the rating, so the same score gain there is worth three times as much.

Bonuses

STEP UP bonus · up to 0.50

Each course you pass adds its bonus once. Passing all 8 courses gives 0.50. Course list

Ladder season bonus · up to 0.50

Play 20 ladder games in a season (ghost games count up to 10) to get ranked, then earn a bonus by rank.

Theoretical maximum

Clearing every chart at 1,000,000 or more gives 12.197 from charts, and 13.197 with every bonus.
