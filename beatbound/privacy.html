2. Online sign-in

Online features start only when you first open ranked or versus play. The game then signs in to Epic Online Services (EOS) with a device-based identifier. You do not create an account, and we never ask for an email address, password or real name. The game passes a sign-in token from EOS to our server (api.memegames.dev), which checks it with Epic and recognizes you by your Product User ID.

3. Advertising (mobile only)

The iPhone, iPad and Android versions show a full-screen ad after every few songs. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies, with the Unity Ads network. These services may collect the information listed in section 1 under their own privacy policies, and may use it for personalized ads where the law and your choices allow.

  • On iPhone and iPad, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking when the system asks. You can change this at any time in Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads in the game. Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never receive your payment card details.

The PC and Mac versions have no ads.

4. Storage and retention

Settings, your nickname and offline records are stored on your device until you delete the game or its data.

On our server we keep:

  • Your position-by-time recording of a song for one day, unless it becomes a ghost or belongs to a match still in progress.
  • Input timing of runs that fail the score check for seven days.
  • Scores, verified input timing, ghosts, ladder and rating records for as long as the online service runs, or until you ask us to delete them. Ghosts are replaced as newer runs come in.
  • Backup copies of the server database for up to 30 days.
  • Web request logs only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting.

We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to handle your request, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. When information is no longer needed, we delete electronic files so they cannot be restored.

5. Service providers and other players

We use the following providers. Each processes information under its own privacy policy.

  • Epic Games, Inc. (Epic Online Services): anonymous sign-in, versus lobbies and P2P connections
  • Oracle Corporation (Oracle Cloud): hosting our game server
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (R2 storage): backups of the server database
  • Unity Technologies: the game engine, in-app purchase handling and, on mobile, LevelPlay and Unity Ads. The Unity engine may also send basic, anonymous device statistics (such as hardware and operating system) to Unity.
  • Apple Inc. and Google LLC: app stores, in-app purchases and payments
  • GitHub, Inc.: hosting this website

Other players see your runs as ghosts with a random generated name and character, a short code derived from your Product User ID, and your ladder points. Leaderboards show only that short code. In a live versus match your opponent receives your Product User ID, generated name, ladder points, rating, equipped character and in-match play. Depending on how players are connected, technical information such as your IP address may be visible to the relay service or to your opponent.

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the ad services described in section 3, we do not share it for advertising.

6. Website

This website is served by GitHub Pages. It has no ads, no analytics scripts and no tracking cookies. GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses to provide and secure its hosting service.

7. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to restrict or object to processing. Send requests to contact@memegames.dev. To help us find your records, include the platform you play on, roughly when you played ranked or versus, and, if you can, your ladder rank or the songs you played. We may ask for more information to confirm the request comes from you.

We reply without undue delay and within the time required by applicable law (for example, one month under the GDPR, or 10 days under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act).

You can play without any online features: if you never open ranked or versus play, the game does not sign in to EOS or contact our server. Deleting the game and its data removes everything stored on your device. On mobile you can also limit ad tracking as described in section 3.

8.2 European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, MeMe Games is the controller of your information. We rely on these legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: online sign-in, ranked play, ghosts, versus matches, the remove-ads purchase
  • Consent: personalized ads and the use of your advertising identifier. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.
  • Legitimate interests: score checks against cheating, security and abuse prevention, non-personalized ads that fund the free mobile version, website hosting. You can object at any time.
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires us to keep or disclose information

You also have the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Score checks are automated, but they only decide whether a run counts for ranking; they have no legal or similarly significant effect on you, and you can contact us if you think a run was rejected by mistake.

8.3 California and other U.S. states

We do not sell personal information. Ad services on mobile may receive identifiers that count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising; you can opt out through your device's ad tracking settings described in section 3. We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. In the past 12 months we collected these categories: identifiers (device-based IDs, advertising identifiers, IP addresses) and internet or other network activity (gameplay data, ad interactions, request logs). You can ask to know, correct or delete your information, directly or through an authorized agent. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

8.4 Republic of Korea

Privacy officer: MeMe Games, contact@memegames.dev. For help with a privacy violation, you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Where a higher age applies (for example under 14 in Korea, or under 16 in some EEA countries), children below that age should use online features only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We collect only what the game needs. Traffic to our server is encrypted (HTTPS), server sessions expire after 12 hours, and access to the server and its backups is restricted. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the game or the law changes and show the new effective date at the top. For significant changes, we will also give notice in the game or on this website.

12. Languages and contact

This policy is written in English and translated into other languages. If a translation differs from the English version, the English version prevails unless local law requires otherwise.
